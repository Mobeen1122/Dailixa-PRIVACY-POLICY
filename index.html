01

Information Collection

Dailixa does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data. We have no servers to send data to, and no accounts to tie your information to.

02

Local Data Storage

All your habit data — streaks, history, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data is never transmitted over any network.

03

Permissions

This app does not request any special device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, or location access. It works completely offline.

04

Third-Party Services

Dailixa does not integrate any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting tools. Your app experience is entirely self-contained.

05

Data Security

Your data resides entirely on your device. The developer has no access to your habit data at any time. If you uninstall the app, all data is removed automatically.

06

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
